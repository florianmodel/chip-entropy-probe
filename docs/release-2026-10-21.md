archiving because its reader expected the obsolete loaded-SPICE `cases` field.
The reader now uses `loaded_cases`, separates unloaded evidence and records
copied-report hashes; three regression tests cover it. The next attempt caught
a capture test running before its physical-simulation fixture existed. That
test now runs after fixture generation. Both fixes are committed in the
selected revision. No silicon RTL or layout change was needed.
