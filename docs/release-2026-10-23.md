## Design change

Each ring's enable now comes from its own registered one-hot `ring_run`
flip-flop, gated only by `ena && rst_n`; the divider reset is their OR. Input-pin
glitches between clock edges can no longer stop or restart a ring, test-mode pins
never reach a ring, and a ring selection change keeps every ring off for at least
one clock period. Monitor warm-up for ring sources starts with the ring, so their
windows complete one clock later. The register map and interface version 2 are
unchanged. Cost: three flip-flops, eight cells in total.
